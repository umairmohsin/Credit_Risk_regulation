## Quick Reference — Key Risk Weights

| Exposure | Best RW | Worst RW | Key Driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sovereign (rated) | **0%** (AAA–AA−) | **150%** (Below B−) | External rating (Table 1) |
| Sovereign (unrated) | **100%** | — | Default |
| Bank ECRA | **20%** (AAA–AA−) | **150%** (Below B−) | Sovereign floor + bank rating |
| Bank SCRA Grade A | **40%** general / **20%** short-term | — | Due diligence |
| Bank SCRA Grade B | **75%** general / **50%** short-term | — | Due diligence |
| Bank SCRA Grade C | **150%** | — | Distress triggers (7.25) |
| Corporate (rated) | **20%** (AAA–AA−) | **150%** (Below BB−) | External rating (Table 6) |
| Corporate (unrated) | **100%** | — | General |
| SME Corporate | **85%** | — | MSME qualifying criteria |
| Retail (qualifying) | **45%** transactor / **75%** other | — | 4 qualifying criteria |
| Residential RE | **20%** (LTV ≤ 50%) | **150%** (ADC) | LTV band (Table 11) |
| Commercial RE | **60%** (LTV ≤ 60%) | **150%** (ADC/unqualifying) | LTV band (Table 12) |
| Equity (non-speculative) | **250%** | — | Holding type |
| Equity (speculative unlisted) | **400%** | — | 7.51 definition |

## How to Use This Site

!!! tip "Finding a clause"
    Use the **search bar** (top right) and type a clause number like `7.29` or a keyword like `SCRA Grade A`.  
    Each clause has an anchor — you can share links like `…/7-4-banks/#clause-7-13`.

!!! note "Footnotes"
    Footnotes appear as expandable **"Footnote [N]"** blocks directly below the clause they annotate.  
    These contain critical qualifying conditions — always expand them before applying a risk weight.

!!! abstract "Diagrams"
    Every clause has a **"How it works"** tab with either a decision matrix (for risk weight lookups)
    or a Mermaid decision flow (for eligibility paths).
